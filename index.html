    About Asquare

    From a pencil line to your jewellery box

    Every Asquare piece passes through the same four hands-on stages in our workshop before it reaches yours.

    1. 01

      Sketch

      Our designers draw each piece by hand, taking cues from temple and antique heirloom jewellery.

    2. 02

      Stone

      American diamond and kemp stones are chosen for sparkle and colour, then hand-set.

    3. 03

      Plate

      Each base is micron gold-plated or rolled-gold layered for a rich, even gold tone.

    4. 04

      Protect

      An anti-tarnish coating seals the finish, and every piece is checked twice before packing.
